Frequently Asked Questions

What is UX design?
UX design stands for User Experience design. It is the process of designing digital or physical products that are easy to use, intuitive, and enjoyable for the user.
What are the key principles of UX design?
The key principles include user-centered design, accessibility, usability, and creating meaningful experiences through intuitive interfaces.
What is the difference between UX and UI design?
While UX focuses on the overall user experience and functionality, UI (User Interface) design focuses on the visual elements and layout of the interface.
What is a wireframe?
A wireframe is a basic visual guide that represents the skeletal framework of a website or application, showing layout and functionality without design elements.
What is user testing?
User testing is the process of evaluating a product by testing it with representative users to identify problems and areas for improvement in the design.
